## Archetype & Playstyle
Defensive control transition. Absorb enemy tanks with heavy single-target counters, then counter-attack with support units down the opposite lane.

## 8-Card Battle Deck
- hog_rider: 30%
- musketeer: 20%
- knight: 5%
- fireball: 10%
- skeletons: 10%
- Giant: 10%
- baby_dragon: 5%
- Pekka: 10%

## Preferred Lane
balanced

## Tactical Triggers (If-Then Rules)
1. IF enemy drops Archer or Goblin barrel -> Cast the fireball directly on the archer or Goblin Barrel of the enemy!
2. IF enemy Princess Tower or King Tower HP < 380 -> Cast Fireball directly onto tower for Crown!
3. IF enemy drops musketeer -> Deploy Skeleton army directly on enemy musketeer after it crosses the bridge 
4. IF Baby Dragon deployed by enemy -> Deploy Musketeer in the middle of our side arena after it crosses the bridge
5. IF Pekka deployed by the enemy -> Deploy Hog Rider on the opposite lane of the lane in which Pekka is deployed
6. IF Pekka is deployed with some other troops or splash troops, then deploy knight and muskteer in the middle of our side of the arena.
7. IF Giant is deployed -> drop Pekka behind it after it crosses the bridge.
8. IF Hog rider is deployed -> Deploy skeletons directly on to the enemy hog rider after it crosses the bridge 
9. IF my elixer is greater than 8 -> Drop Hog rider and Baby dragon in the side of the enemy princess tower with the least HP
