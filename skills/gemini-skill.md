# Deck Name: The Iron Boar Battalion

# Player / Author: Team Apex

# War Cry: "Steel and swine, victory is mine!"

## Archetype & Playstyle

pekka_control - Focuses on using a heavy armored juggernaut to completely shut down massive enemy pushes, then immediately transitioning into a fast counter-attack by rushing the bridge with a fast win-condition alongside surviving defensive units.

## 8-Card Battle Deck

pekka: 20%
hog_rider: 20%
fireball: 15%
musketeer: 15%
baby_dragon: 10%
knight: 10%
archers: 5%
skeletons: 5%

## Preferred Lane

balanced

## Tactical Triggers (If-Then Rules)

1. IF the enemy deploys a heavy tank -> Deploy pekka to instantly shut down their push!
2. IF you successfully defend an attack with surviving troops -> Drop hog_rider at the bridge for a massive counter-attack!
3. IF the opponent clusters fragile ranged units or buildings together -> Cast fireball for instant area damage!
4. IF a high-damage, single-target melee unit crosses the bridge -> Drop skeletons in the center to pull and distract them!
