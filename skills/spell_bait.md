# Deck Name: Goblin Barrel Spell Bait
# Player / Author: Princess Tricky
# War Cry: "Can't touch this!"

## Archetype & Playstyle
Fast-paced bait and punish. Pressure the enemy with relentless swarms and cheap troops to force them to expend their spells. The moment their defense is on cooldown, launch the Goblin Barrel directly onto their Princess Tower for devastating dagger strikes.

## 8-Card Battle Deck
- goblin_barrel: 35% (Throws 3 stab goblins directly onto the enemy tower)
- knight: 20% (Sturdy tank that absorbs tower fire while goblins stab)
- skeletons: 15% (Distraction army to bait enemy spells and stop tanks)
- archers: 15% (Defensive backline snipers)
- fireball: 10% (Tower finisher and crowd control)
- musketeer: 5% (Air defense)
- baby_dragon: 0%
- hog_rider: 0%

## Preferred Lane
right (Focus all barrel strikes onto the right Princess Tower)

## Tactical Triggers (If-Then Rules)
1. IF Knight is tanking tower shots -> Launch Goblin Barrel onto that tower immediately!
2. IF enemy plays a heavy tank -> Drop Skeletons and Archers in center to stall.
3. IF enemy Princess Tower HP < 300 -> Cast Fireball to secure the Crown!
