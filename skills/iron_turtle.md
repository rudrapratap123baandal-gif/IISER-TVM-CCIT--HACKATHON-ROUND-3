# Deck Name: Iron Bastion Fortress
# Player / Author: Stoneguard
# War Cry: "None shall breach the impenetrable wall!"

## Archetype & Playstyle
Defensive fortress into Giant steamroller. Let the enemy exhaust their elixir attacking our Princess Towers into Musketeer and Archer fire. Once enemy elixir is drained, deploy Giant with Baby Dragon behind it for an unstoppable counter-push.

## 8-Card Battle Deck
- giant: 30% (Late-game siege tank)
- musketeer: 25% (Heavy backline tower defense)
- archers: 15% (Defensive archer pair)
- knight: 15% (Solid brawler holding the bridge)
- fireball: 10% (Spell defense)
- skeletons: 5% (Distraction swarm)
- baby_dragon: 0%
- hog_rider: 0%

## Preferred Lane
right (Hold and push the right bridge)

## Tactical Triggers (If-Then Rules)
1. IF enemy approaches our Princess Tower -> Defend with Musketeer and Skeletons!
2. IF Elixir reaches 9 -> Drop Giant in back to start a massive push!
3. IF Double Elixir is active -> Stack Musketeers and Baby Dragon behind Giant!
