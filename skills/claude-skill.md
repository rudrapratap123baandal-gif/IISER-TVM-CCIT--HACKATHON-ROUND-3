# Deck Name: Iron Bridge Raiders
# Player / Author: Team Bridge Breakers
# War Cry: "No tower stands past the third cycle!"

## Archetype & Playstyle
bridge_spam with a cycle backbone. Keep the elixir bar low with cheap cards
(skeletons, knight, archers) and pressure both lanes with hog_rider and
goblin_barrel. Fireball clears support troops and finishes damaged towers.
Musketeer and baby_dragon defend, then counter-push.

## 8-Card Battle Deck
- hog_rider: 18%
- knight: 14%
- musketeer: 14%
- archers: 12%
- goblin_barrel: 12%
- fireball: 12%
- baby_dragon: 10%
- skeletons: 8%

## Preferred Lane
right

## Tactical Triggers (If-Then Rules)
1. IF the enemy spends 5+ elixir on a push -> counter with hog_rider at the bridge and cycle skeletons!
2. IF an enemy tower is below ~400 HP -> finish it with fireball or a goblin_barrel drop!
3. IF the enemy plays a ground tank -> distract it with skeletons and hit it with musketeer and archers behind knight!
4. IF the enemy clumps support troops behind a tank -> fireball them, then counter-push with baby_dragon!
