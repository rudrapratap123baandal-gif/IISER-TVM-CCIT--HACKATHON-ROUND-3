# Deck Name: Berserker Blitz Bridge Spam
# Player / Author: Crimson Horde
# War Cry: "Crush them before they draw breath!"

## Archetype & Playstyle
Ultra-aggressive bridge rush. Drop Hog Rider and Knight at the bridges the instant elixir is available. Never sit on full elixir. Push both lanes relentlessly to overwhelm the enemy before they can deploy slow tanks.

## 8-Card Battle Deck
- hog_rider: 35% (Non-stop bridge assaults)
- knight: 25% (Aggressive melee brawler)
- baby_dragon: 15% (Air splash support)
- musketeer: 10% (Long-range suppressive fire)
- fireball: 10% (Spell strikes on towers)
- skeletons: 5% (Distraction cycle)
- archers: 0%
- giant: 0%

## Preferred Lane
left (Direct rush on the left Princess Tower)

## Tactical Triggers (If-Then Rules)
1. IF Elixir >= 4 -> Deploy Hog Rider or Knight immediately at the left bridge!
2. IF enemy drops Giant -> Counter-push on the opposite lane!
3. IF enemy King Tower HP < 800 -> Launch all troops straight for the 3-Crown KO!
