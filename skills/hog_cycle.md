# Deck Name: Hog Rider 2.6 Cycle
# Player / Author: The Hog Master
# War Cry: "Hoooooog Riiiiiiider!"

## Archetype & Playstyle
Fast cycle beatdown. Keep elixir costs lean and relentlessly deploy Hog Rider on the left or right bridge whenever the enemy overcommits. Defend opposing tanks with Skeletons and Musketeer, then turn defense into instant counter-attacks!

## 8-Card Battle Deck
- hog_rider: 40% (Primary win condition - charges directly at towers)
- musketeer: 20% (Heavy ranged defense against aerial and ground tanks)
- knight: 15% (Cheap mini-tank brawler to protect snipers)
- skeletons: 10% (Cheap 2-elixir swarm to distract giants and pekkas)
- fireball: 10% (Direct spell strike to clear clusters or finish towers)
- archers: 5% (Support fire from the bridge)
- baby_dragon: 0%
- goblin_barrel: 0%

## Preferred Lane
balanced (Exploits whichever lane the opponent leaves undefended)

## Tactical Triggers (If-Then Rules)
1. IF Elixir >= 4 and enemy lane is clear -> Immediately deploy Hog Rider at the bridge!
2. IF enemy deploys Giant or Pekka -> Drop Skeletons in center to distract, with Musketeer behind!
3. IF enemy Princess Tower HP < 250 -> Cast Fireball directly onto the tower to claim the Crown!
4. IF enemy attacks left lane -> Counter-attack with Hog Rider on the opposite right lane!
