# Deck Name: P.E.K.K.A Iron Control
# Player / Author: Mecha Warlord
# War Cry: "Butterflies of Destruction!"

## Archetype & Playstyle
Impenetrable defense into catastrophic counter-push. Deploy P.E.K.K.A to single-handedly destroy any enemy tank attempting to cross the bridge. Once the defense succeeds, support P.E.K.K.A with Baby Dragon and Musketeer to bulldoze all remaining enemy towers.

## 8-Card Battle Deck
- pekka: 35% (Massive armored powerhouse dealing 460 damage per swing)
- baby_dragon: 20% (Splash support clearing swarms in front of Pekka)
- musketeer: 15% (Heavy backline ranged defense)
- knight: 10% (Mid-field mini tank)
- fireball: 10% (Area denial)
- skeletons: 5% (Cycle distraction)
- archers: 5% (Defensive archer pair)
- hog_rider: 0%

## Preferred Lane
balanced (Deploy Pekka directly on whichever lane the enemy attempts to attack)

## Tactical Triggers (If-Then Rules)
1. IF enemy drops Giant or Hog Rider -> Drop P.E.K.K.A right in front of the bridge!
2. IF P.E.K.K.A is counter-pushing -> Drop Baby Dragon behind her to incinerate swarms!
3. IF Double Elixir triggers (last 60s) -> Ramp up aggression and overwhelm with dual-tank pushes!
