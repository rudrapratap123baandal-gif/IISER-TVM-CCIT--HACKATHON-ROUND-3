# Deck Name: Giant Beatdown Heavy
# Player / Author: Mountain King
# War Cry: "Crush the Crown!"

## Archetype & Playstyle
Slow building steamroller push. Accumulate elixir until near full, then drop the colossal Giant at the back of the left lane. Stack high-damage ranged troops (Musketeer, Baby Dragon, Archers) directly behind the Giant so they shred opposing defenses while the Giant tanks tower damage.

## 8-Card Battle Deck
- giant: 35% (Massive 2200 HP siege tank targeting buildings only)
- baby_dragon: 20% (Flying splash damage to vaporize enemy swarm defense)
- musketeer: 20% (Sniper dealing massive single-target damage behind Giant)
- knight: 10% (Secondary mini-tank for counter-defense)
- fireball: 10% (Spell blast to clear enemy defenses clustered around Giant)
- archers: 5% (Pair of ranged snipers)
- skeletons: 0%
- hog_rider: 0%

## Preferred Lane
left (Build an unstoppable death-ball down the left lane)

## Tactical Triggers (If-Then Rules)
1. IF Elixir >= 8 -> Deploy Giant at the back of the left lane!
2. IF Giant crosses river -> Immediately support with Baby Dragon and Musketeer!
3. IF enemy swarms our Giant with Skeletons -> Fireball or let Baby Dragon splash them!
4. IF enemy King Tower is exposed -> Push all remaining troops into the center for the 3-Crown KO!
