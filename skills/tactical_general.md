# Deck Name: Imperial Vanguard Balanced
# Player / Author: General Valerius
# War Cry: "Discipline, precision, and overwhelming victory!"

## Archetype & Playstyle
Disciplined combined arms. Balanced elixir curve with Knight, Archers, Musketeer, and Giant. Adapts to whichever lane has the advantage.

## 8-Card Battle Deck
- giant: 25% (Frontline siege tank)
- musketeer: 20% (Heavy sniper)
- knight: 20% (Melee brawler)
- archers: 15% (Ranged support)
- hog_rider: 10% (Punish rusher)
- fireball: 10% (Direct spell damage)
- baby_dragon: 0%
- skeletons: 0%

## Preferred Lane
balanced

## Tactical Triggers (If-Then Rules)
1. IF defending -> Use Knight to tank and Musketeer to snipe!
2. IF counter-pushing -> Drop Giant at bridge in front of surviving troops!
