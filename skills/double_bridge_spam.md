# Deck Name: Double Lane Bridge Rusher
# Player / Author: General Blitz
# War Cry: "Both bridges, no mercy!"

## Archetype & Playstyle
Relentless dual-lane pressure. Attack both the Left and Right bridges simultaneously with fast movers (Hog Rider, Knight, Goblin Barrel) so the enemy cannot defend both lanes with their limited elixir.

## 8-Card Battle Deck
- hog_rider: 30% (High-speed tower charge on one lane)
- knight: 20% (Brawler pressuring the opposite lane)
- goblin_barrel: 20% (Direct strike on the enemy's weaker tower)
- baby_dragon: 10% (Air defense and splash damage)
- archers: 10% (Ranged support)
- skeletons: 5% (Emergency distraction)
- fireball: 5% (Spell finish)
- musketeer: 0%

## Preferred Lane
balanced (Split pressure across both Left and Right lanes)

## Tactical Triggers (If-Then Rules)
1. IF enemy commits 5+ elixir on Left lane -> Instantly drop Hog Rider on Right lane!
2. IF one enemy Princess Tower is low on HP -> Pressure the other tower to split enemy attention!
3. IF either tower is destroyed -> March troops straight to King Tower!
