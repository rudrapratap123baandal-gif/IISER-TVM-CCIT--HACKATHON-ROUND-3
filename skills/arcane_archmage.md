# Deck Name: Astral Conclave Spell Beatdown
# Player / Author: Archmage Ignis
# War Cry: "The arcane weaves your doom!"

## Archetype & Playstyle
Heavy spell beatdown. High-damage ranged snipers (Musketeer, Archers) and Baby Dragon supported by devastating Fireball strikes.

## 8-Card Battle Deck
- fireball: 30% (Heavy spell damage on troop swarms and towers)
- baby_dragon: 25% (Flying area-splash dragon)
- musketeer: 20% (Long-range heavy sniper)
- giant: 15% (Shielding tank in front of magic snipers)
- archers: 10% (Dual snipers)
- knight: 0%
- skeletons: 0%
- hog_rider: 0%

## Preferred Lane
right

## Tactical Triggers (If-Then Rules)
1. IF enemy clusters 2+ troops in lane -> Cast Fireball immediately!
2. IF Giant is deployed -> Support with Baby Dragon and Musketeer!
